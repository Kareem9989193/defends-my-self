Section II Technical Dossier: The 6 Constitutional Loopholes & Remediation Amendments

1. The Catastrophic Loophole: Explicit Legalization of Real Nude Media

Critical Vulnerability
«No member shall create, publish, share, distribute... any AI-generated nude image...
But if these nudes are real, it is allowed to use them and send them on the server.» (Line 54)
Vulnerability & Severe Risk: While the drafters intended to restrict artificial intelligence tools, they drafted an exception that literally and explicitly authorizes distributing real nude images of server members. Under the current text, any member sharing real non-consensual nude pictures possesses an explicit constitutional shield against punishment!
Recommended Constitutional Amendment: Immediately strike out line 54 and amend the article to state: «No member shall under any circumstance create, publish, share, or circulate any nude media—whether authentic or AI-generated—under penalty of immediate permanent ban.»

2. The Factual Judgment Principle: Invalidation of Subjective Presumptions

Immediate Defense Shield
«Principle of Factual Judgment: Decisions and penalties shall be based solely on what actually occurred, the established facts, and the available evidence, rather than presumed intentions or personal interpretations. Violations shall be judged according to the actions that took place, not what a person is assumed to have intended.»
Vulnerability & Risk: This core principle renders any administrative penalty based on "assumed intent" or "promoting personal interests" null and void unless the administration provides concrete, physical evidence of tangible harm and actual commercial promotion.
Recommended Constitutional Amendment: Redraft the provision to permit judges reasonable discretion to weigh corroborated circumstantial evidence rather than solely tangible proof.

3. Exclusivity of Sanctions: Illegitimacy of "Clip / Media Bans"

Ultra Vires Penalty
Article I strictly enumerates only two penalties for soundboard deletion:
1. Permanent prohibition from submitting any new soundboards for acceptance.
2. Adding a personal soundboard associated with the offender as an additional penalty.
Vulnerability & Risk: The Constitution contains zero authorization for banning clips or media uploads. Imposing a "clip ban" constitutes an ultra vires sanction lacking constitutional basis, directly violating the doctrine of legality ("No penalty without prior constitutional text").
Recommended Constitutional Amendment: Introduce an explicit clause in Article I granting the administration the power to impose proportional ancillary penalties, including clip upload restrictions.

4. Ambiguity of the "Authorization" Requirement (Article I, Clause 1)

Administrative Ambiguity
«No member may delete any soundboard without authorization.»
Vulnerability & Risk: The text fails to designate who holds the authority to grant authorization. When a member is technically granted Discord management permissions without a written prohibition, possession of the technical permission constitutes implicit institutional authorization.
Recommended Constitutional Amendment: Specify the clause as: «without prior written ticket authorization approved by Senior Management.»

5. The Universal Immunity Shield: Article VI's "Ordinary Banter" Defense

Absolute Defense Defense
«Friendly jokes, ordinary banter, respectful disagreement, and good-faith criticism shall not constitute violations.»
Vulnerability & Risk: This language is overly expansive. Any member facing disciplinary charges over disruptive language, controversial nicknames (e.g., "ya co-boss"), or audio files can invoke this clause as an absolute defense, legally binding judges to dismiss charges.
Recommended Constitutional Amendment: Add a qualifying clause: «Provided that such humor does not infringe upon personal honor, institutional security, or targeted harassment.»

6. The Dual-Text Contradiction & Unannounced Constitutional Amendments

Constitutional Invalidation
«Final Provision: This Constitution shall serve as the official governing document of GC... No amendments shall be made unless officially announced.»
Vulnerability & Risk: The official Arabic version terminates at the Final Provision, while the English version contains substantial unannounced addenda. Unannounced amendments are constitutionally null; moreover, conflicting official drafts must legally be resolved in favor of the accused under standard legal doctrine.
